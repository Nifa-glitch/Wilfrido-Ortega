Investigashon

Bibliografia, publikashon i fuentenan

E bibliografia a registrá tur título ku a topa i den ki forma nan a keda publiká. A konta título, tomo di seri, kuentanan ku ta relatá na otro i vershonnan di republikashon; stencil, kaft duru, etc.

Pa a yega na e bibliografia aki a hasi un investigashon digital pa konsultá katalogo di diferente organisashon. Pa verifiká ku e publikashonnan ta eksistí a bishita e organisashon, a kontrolá kolekshonnan privá i papia ku hende.

Research

Bibliography, publishing history and sources

The bibliography records every title located and the forms in which it was published. The research counts titles, volumes within series, related narratives, and republication formats, including stenciled copies and hardback editions.

To compile this bibliography, digital research was conducted using the catalogues of various organizations. To verify that the publications exist, the researcher visited institutions, checked private collections, and spoke with people.

03 · CONNECTIONS

Relationships between the works

Wilfrido wrote several series. Some carried the same title across different volumes. Others used different titles, with Ortega himself indicating which work followed next.

  • Su segundo amor → Sanger ta yama sanger → Bo no por hui pa bo destino
  • Splendor di solo → Shimaron
  • Konvenio di amor → E kombenio di amor (adaptation)
