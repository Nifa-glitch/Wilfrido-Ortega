Wilfrido
Ortega.

Wilfrido Ortega was born on 26 October 1932 and died on 26 May 2026 at the age of 93. He devoted himself to writing and publishing books in Papiamentu. This website explores his literary output, his ingenuity in publishing, and how he personally distributed his books door to door.

AN EARLY PUBLICATION

Destino di Amor

The first manuscript of Destino di Amor contained 500 pages. On the recommendation of Otto Zalm of Editorial Tim Americana, Wilfrido reduced it to sixty pages to lower printing costs.
