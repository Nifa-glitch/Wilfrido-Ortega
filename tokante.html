Tokante.

E bibliografia aki ta parti di un proseso pa investigá trabou di e outor Wilfrido Ortega. Apesar di ta menshoná diferente biaha komo un di e outornan mas produktivo di Kòrsou, no ta tin un lista kompletu, aksesibel, di kiko tur Wilfrido a produsí.

01 / ORIGEN

Motivashon

1958 — 2013

Kórsou ta tin mas outor ku a bende nan bukinan di porta pa porta i ku a skibi novelita. Amador Nita, Tuyuchi, Guillermo Rosario, Nilo Kleinmoedig, Edward de Jongh ta algun di nan. E echo ku Wilfrido a skibi solamente na Papiamentu, a publiká, ilustrá i a distribuí su bukinan di un forma struktural for di 1958 te ku 2013, ta un di e rasonnan. E falta di informashon ku tin di e trabou di Wilfrido Ortega, esei a chispa e kuriosidat.

E bibliografia ta un instrumento pa sigui investigá.
02 / PREGUNTANAN

Meta

Aparte di duna un bista den e produktividat di Wilfrido, Nifa ke kontestá diferente pregunta mas;

01

Kiko tabata e balor di Wilfrido Ortega su trabou pa desaroyo di idioma i literatura Papiamentu?

02

Ki impakto Wilfrido su konsistensia ta tin pa e komunidat di Kòrsou?

03

Kiko di kombibensia di tur tempu por saka for di e storianan di Wilfrido?

Pa por hasi esaki mester a kompilá un bibliografia di e publikashonnan di Wilfrido i tambe sa na unda por haña nan.

WILFRIDO ORTEGA · ARCHIVO LITERARIO

Tokante.

This bibliography is part of an ongoing investigation into the work of author Wilfrido Ortega. Although he has often been described as one of Curaçao’s most prolific writers, there is no complete, accessible list of everything he produced.

01—04
The research behind the bibliography
Motivation · Aims · Learning · Nifa Ansano
WO
01 / ORIGINS

Motivation

1958 — 2013

Curaçao has other authors who sold books door to door and wrote short novels. Amador Nita, Tuyuchi, Guillermo Rosario, Nilo Kleinmoedig and Edward de Jongh are among them. One reason for this research is that Wilfrido wrote only in Papiamentu and published, illustrated and distributed his books consistently from 1958 to 2013. The lack of information about his work sparked this curiosity.

The bibliography is a tool for further research.
02 / QUESTIONS

Aims

Besides providing an overview of Wilfrido’s productivity, Nifa seeks to answer several other questions:

01

What was the value of Wilfrido Ortega’s work for the development of the Papiamentu language and literature?

02

What impact did Wilfrido’s consistency have on Curaçao’s community?

03

What can his stories tell us about life and social relations across different periods?

To explore these questions, it was necessary to compile a bibliography of Wilfrido’s publications and identify where they can be found.

03 / LEARNING

Programmes that contributed

This digital bibliography grew out of several professional development programmes in which Nifa has participated in recent years:

01

Each One Teach One

Each One Teach One, a programme of Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie, focuses on developing editing and publishing skills. During the programme, attention was given to the publication ‘Destino di Amor’ written by Ortega.

02

Caribbean Digital Scholarship
Summer Institute

The Caribbean Digital Scholarship Summer Institute focuses on digital publishing. Among other things, the programme involved working with GitHub to present archival material. Cultuurfonds Caribisch Gebied co-financed the trip to Jamaica for Digital Caribbean as part of the Digital Connections project.

04 / RESEARCHER

Nifa Ansano

Nifa Ansano is a writer who is curious about the subjects she explores, especially Curaçao’s history, and is passionate about literature. She has contributed to various projects promoting Curaçaoan authors and literature. She currently works at Fundashon NAAM and is treasurer of patruli.org, a platform promoting literature in the diaspora.
