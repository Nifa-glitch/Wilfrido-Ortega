WILFRIDO ORTEGA · ARCHIVO LITERARIO

Su produktividat

01 · LITERATURA

Literatura

Wilfrido ta konosí pa su novelanan. Mayoria ta novela romantiko manera e mes a yamanan òf novelita instruktivo, òf novela historiko.

Durante e investigashon a deskubrí algun buki di poesia di Wilfrido. Den algun publikashon e tabata kuminsá ku un kantika. I aki aya por a topa ‘Kos di hari’, un chiste kórtiku komo ñapa.

02 · TEATER

Teater

Ta e novelanan el a transformá den obranan di teater. Guiermito di Shon trei, e Yonkuman ku brio ta dos teksto ku el a presentá komo obra teatral na Boneiru. Raphael Rodriguez ku a aktua den e obranan, a konfirmá esaki.

Na Kòrsou e ta tin mas ku un grupo, un di nan tabata yama AKRIDWO. E nifikashon di e abreviashon no ta konosí ainda.

GRUPONAN TEATRAL

AKRIDWO & BONADWO

Na Boneiru e ta tin e grupo ku el a yama BONADWO, Bon Aktornan Di Wilfrido Ortega.

Papi Adriana, Tio Ali i Boi Antoine, ta algun persona ku a aktua den mas ku un obra di Wilfrido Ortega.

E nifikashon di AKRIDWO no ta konosí ainda.
03 · RADIONOVELA

Radionovela

Radionovela tambe tabata un adaptashon di novelanan ku Wilfrido a skibi.

El a transformá e novela E konvenio di amor ku a pasa na Radio Z86. Protagonistanan di e radionovela tabata: Gilbert Sparren, Chea Gummarus, Marisela Cratz, Crista Toppenberg, Suzy Adamus, Ludgarda Noor, Shardira Cijntje. Mildred Rooi, Norwin Leonora, Tom Toppenberg, Maruta Arindel, Lucienne i Anthony Elisa. Pietje Willems a nara e novela, Gibi Doran a interpretá e ‘jingle.

Radio Z86 · E konvenio di amor

Originalmente a hasi uzo di e espasio na Fundashon Pro Bista pa por a graba e radionovela. David Ariza a asistí pa digitalisá e material pa a pase na radio.

WILFRIDO ORTEGA · ARCHIVO LITERARIO

His productivity

01 · LITERATURE

Literature

Wilfrido is known for his novels. Most are romantic novels, as he called them himself, or instructional novellas or historical novels.

During the research, several books of poetry by Wilfrido were discovered. Some publications opened with a song. Here and there, readers could also find “Kos di hari,” a short joke added as a little extra.

02 · THEATRE

Theatre

He also adapted his novels into theatrical works. Guiermito di Shon trei and Yonkuman ku brio are two texts he presented as theatre on Bonaire. Raphael Rodriguez, who acted in these works, confirmed this.

On Curaçao he had more than one group; one was called AKRIDWO. The meaning of the abbreviation is not yet known.

THEATRE GROUPS

AKRIDWO & BONADWO

On Bonaire, he had a group he called BONADWO, short for Bon Aktornan Di Wilfrido Ortega.

Papi Adriana, Tio Ali and Boi Antoine are among those who acted in more than one of Wilfrido Ortega’s productions.

The meaning of AKRIDWO is not yet known.
03 · RADIONOVELA

Radio drama

Radio dramas were also adaptations of novels written by Wilfrido.

He adapted the novel E konvenio di amor, which was broadcast on Radio Z86. The radio drama featured Gilbert Sparren, Chea Gummarus, Marisela Cratz, Crista Toppenberg, Suzy Adamus, Ludgarda Noor, Shardira Cijntje, Mildred Rooi, Norwin Leonora, Tom Toppenberg, Maruta Arindel, Lucienne and Anthony Elisa. Pietje Willems narrated the drama, and Gibi Doran performed the jingle.
